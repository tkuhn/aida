AIDA
Scheme URIs In use Publications GitHub

A scheme to identify and link claims and hypotheses.

Each one is written as a single English sentence, and that sentence becomes its identifier. The sentence is:

  1. A

    Atomic

    A sentence describing one thought that cannot be further broken down in a practical way.

  2. I

    Independent

    A sentence that can stand on its own, without external references like “this effect” or “we”.

  3. D

    Declarative

    A complete sentence ending with a full stop that could in theory be either true or false.

  4. A

    Absolute

    A sentence describing the core of a claim, ignoring the (un)certainty about its truth and how it was discovered: no “probably” or “evaluation showed that”. Typically in present tense.

Every AIDA sentence has a URI

Malaria is transmitted by mosquitoes.

http://purl.org/aida/Malaria+is+transmitted+by+mosquitoes.

The URI is the prefix http://purl.org/aida/ followed by the URL-encoded sentence, with spaces written as +.

The same sentence always gives the same URI.
